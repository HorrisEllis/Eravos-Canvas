ERAVOS v0.2.7 (2026-11-05)

Install: ERAVOS-Setup-0.2.7.exe, or unzip ERAVOS_0.2.7.zip. On an earlier version, ERAVOS offers this update by itself.

Piano roll
- Right-click selected notes › ✎ Edit note… opens the note editor (it opened only on a double-click).
- The note editor gains Chance (how often it plays; the same passes every time, so a song stays the same), Ratchet (2–8 fast hits)
  and Nudge (push it early or late, up to half a step). A note that plays by chance is drawn paler; a ratchet shows its hits.
- Fixed: the Bend tool's dive / rise drag on a note's end did nothing in 0.2.6.

ERAVOS v0.2.6 (2026-11-05)

Install: ERAVOS-Setup-0.2.6.exe, or unzip ERAVOS_0.2.6.zip. On an earlier version, ERAVOS offers this update by itself.

Piano roll
- You hear the notes as you draw them, even on a track with no instrument yet (a small preview voice), and on every new pitch while
  you drag one.
- Shape a note with points (〰 Bend tool, one note selected): click the middle of a note to add a point and drag it; drag any point
  (Shift: whole semitones); double-click a point to remove it. The ends still dive / rise as before.
- Zoom in much further for precision: Zoom up to 8XL, Rows up to 4XL (Ctrl+wheel / Ctrl+Shift+wheel go there too).

ERAVOS v0.2.5 (2026-11-05)

Install: ERAVOS-Setup-0.2.5.exe, or unzip ERAVOS_0.2.5.zip. On an earlier version, ERAVOS offers this update by itself.

Fixed
- The hundreds of "art.svg 404" errors: every window asked for its painting at the wrong address.
- Ctrl+A selects every note again wherever the piano roll has focus or the pointer (it only worked with the grid focused).

Piano roll

Timeline

- Right-click menus are bigger and easier to hit.

Canvas and Settings

- Updates download in the background; ERAVOS Update puts them in place, keeps a backup, and restarts.

ERAVOS v0.2.4 (2026-11-05)

Install: ERAVOS-Setup-0.2.4.exe, or unzip ERAVOS_0.2.4.zip. On an earlier version, ERAVOS offers this update by itself.

Piano roll

ERAVOS v0.2.0 (2026-11-05)

Install: run ERAVOS-Setup-0.2.0.exe (Program Files, Start menu, Settings > Apps), or unzip ERAVOS_0.2.0.zip and run ERAVOS.exe.
Already on 0.1.0? ERAVOS offers this update by itself; your work in Documents\ERAVOS is not touched.

What is new
- Black surfaces: every surface is neutral black; only colour is colour. No tint, no cast, in every theme.
- Reactive colours > Beat: every tool, the toolbar and tool pages share one colour that moves on the beat (every beat, 2 beats or bar).
- Tool pages cleaned up: one size for every tab, a compact head, presets in full, the controls as cards, inputs and outputs in words.
- Consistency across every tool: one kit (button, toggle, knob, segment) at the kit's sizes, one main action per window, each tool's
  gradients in its own colour (Tape Echo's Throw), knob labels that never run together.
- Your library is Documents\ERAVOS (projects, exports, packs, tools, drop); the installer puts ERAVOS in Program Files.
- Delete packs, presets, kits and patterns for good.
- Wobble Bass and Wub Filter share one vowel filter: Wobble Bass gains YOI, Wub Filter gains UH, IH and ER.
- A new state-variable filter in the engine (for the tools to come), proved on its own.

Under the hood
- A design guide (docs/DESIGN-GUIDE.md) and a design check that measures every tool on every change.
- No toys: a check that every instrument makes its sound live, with no shipped recordings.
- The canvas and the content stay separate: canvas/ is the host and knows no tool; tools/ holds the tools, packs and suites.

ERAVOS v0.1.0 — first tagged release (2026-10-24)

The first numbered release. (Builds before this were named by their map phase, e.g. 3.36.0-alpha; from here on
releases are v0.x.y.) Run it the same way: double-click "Open ERAVOS.html", or Start ERAVOS.bat with Node.js, or
build ERAVOS.exe with "Build ERAVOS.exe.bat".

What is in it
- A canvas of instruments, effects and arrangement tools: Timeline with a piano roll (per-note settings, per-note
  curves with Bézier handles, note behaviours, pitch bends), Song Recorder (export WAV/FLAC/AIFF/MP3/Opus and stems,
  with tags and a cover — autofilled, or upload your own image), Session, Mixer.
- Suites — tools that come as a system, each with its own page and macros: Heavy Drops, Wub Lab, Synthesis Lab,
  Synthwave, Gothic, Sound System, Xen.
- The Wobble Bass family: Wobble Bass, Wub Osc / Filter / Rate (speed bend, glide, rate input) / Sub, Squash (three-
  band OTT-style squash), Destroyer, Drop Builder, Sub Engine.
- Songbooks: songs by genre, including Floor Falls (8 deep-drop songs) and Heavy Drops; a pattern library.
- Right-click menus on the canvas and on every tool.
- Reactive colours (Options → Reactive colours): the accent colours follow the sound — the app's with the mix, each
  tool's with its own output; every colour keeps its own hue.
- Drawn icons throughout; tool and suite pages open from + Add.

Known limits
- Per-note curves are monophonic-focused: overlapping notes on one instrument share its knobs.
- Opus exports carry no tags.

ERAVOS 3.36.0-alpha — one zip (everything except the unchanged Electron runtime)

FASTEST: double-click "Open ERAVOS.html" (use Edge or Chrome). Nothing to install. Your StarryPad works there.
  For the most reliable saving/microphone: install Node.js (LTS) once, then double-click source\Start ERAVOS.bat.
  Want the stand-alone ERAVOS.exe and the complete one-file zip? Double-click "Make ERAVOS.exe (Windows).bat".
  It downloads the official Electron runtime once (~110 MB), builds ERAVOS\ERAVOS.exe with all 47 tools, writes
  ERAVOS_3.36.0-alpha_complete.zip next to this folder, and starts ERAVOS. No Node.js needed.

ALREADY HAVE AN ERAVOS.exe FOLDER (Windows)
  Copy the folder  windows-x64\ERAVOS  from this zip over your existing ERAVOS folder (the one with ERAVOS.exe)
  and choose "Replace". That updates resources\app (the canvas) and ERAVOS Library\Mods\Music (all the tools).
  ERAVOS.exe itself is unchanged: it is only the empty canvas; every tool comes from the packs.

RUN WITHOUT THE EXE (any browser)
  source\Start ERAVOS.bat (Windows) or source/start-eravos.sh — opens http://localhost:4747. Needs Node.js once.
  Rebuild a complete ERAVOS.exe from source: node source/desktop/build.mjs
