# The landmark songs of each style — their techniques, for ERAVOS's deep songs

For `specs/eravos.deep-songs.spec`. His words (2026-11-14): "can you look at every major song of all synthwave, edm, dubstep, electronic, etc
to make a wide contrast of songs per catagory that are as complex and deep as eravos can be. like per note edits, automation, tools wired to
other tools, etc. curves, etc. as deep as possiblr"

**Technique only.** What is taken from a record is how it is made: its tempo and groove, its form, its sound design moves, what moves while it
plays. No melody, lyric, chord sequence or sample of any record is used (the rule since 3.36: "technique not copies"). Tempos are approximate.
Written from listening knowledge of these records; where a fact is uncertain it is said. One ERAVOS song per row, its own sub-style, so a
shelf holds six very different songs instead of variations on one arc.

## 🏠 House · Garage · 🌀 Techno · Trance · 🎛 Trap · Future (next batch)

- Daft Punk — One More Time / Stardust — Music Sounds Better: filter house — the whole loop through a filter that opens over minutes; sidechain pump.
- Avicii — Levels · Swedish House Mafia: progressive anthem — plucked lead, long breakdown, chord lift, white-noise riser.

## What every deep song uses (the checklist, from these techniques)

Per note: a knob value set on the note (`p`), a knob curve inside the note (`c`), a bend with its own LFO (`b.osc`), a chord from one note,
strummed (`x.iv`), plays only on pass k of every N (`x.cond`), chance (`pr`), ratchets that speed up or slow down (`rt`, `x.rs`, `x.rv`),
microtiming (`mt`: swing, a late snare), humanize per pass (`x.ht`, `x.hv`). Curves on the instruments, on effects and on modulators. An
LFO and the XY Pad wired into knobs; a tool or an effect driving another tool (the sidechain's envelope, the Swarm's coherence, Tape
Memory's dropouts, Dynamics' gain reduction, Bass Forge's talk LFO). Effects chained. Each song its own form, tempo, groove and palette.
