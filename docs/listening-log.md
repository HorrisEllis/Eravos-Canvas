# Listening log — what the ear decides

> Sent by him (2026-11-07T10:26:25Z): "Novelty isn’t the same as quality. A preset can be novel but sound terrible. Another can resemble an
> existing preset but be substantially more useful. You need separate measures for: Novelty · Musical usefulness · Expressiveness ·
> Controllability · Functional distinctiveness" · "don’t let an immature measurement system become an authority over creativity."

The novelty gate (`build/novelty-gate.mjs`) measures **one** of the five: how far a sound is from everything ERAVOS already has. The other four
are heard. They are logged here, one line per listen, never edited after (a later listen is a new line). The gate's numbers sit beside them so
the measure can be checked against the ear: when the ear and the number disagree, the ear is right and the measure is what gets fixed.

**Each column, 1–5 or blank (not heard yet):**
- **Usefulness:** would you reach for it in a track?
- **Expressiveness:** does it answer playing (velocity, held notes, modulation)?
- **Controllability:** do its controls take it somewhere wanted, without getting lost?
- **Distinct role:** does it do a job nothing else in ERAVOS does?
